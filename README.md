# Hotel-Booking-And-Travel-Revenue-Analysis
Hotel Booking and Travel Revenue Analysis dashboard built with Tableau to analyze booking trend, stay categories, origin and destination countries, payment method, age group and top destination cities
 Hotel Booking and Travel Revenue Analysis

An interactive dashboard analyzing global hotel booking patterns, traveler demographics, and revenue trends from 2010–2019, built to uncover insights into stay behavior, origin/destination markets, and payment preferences in the hospitality industry.

Key Insights

*Booking Trend*
- Bookings grew steadily from 4,798 in 2010 to 9,502 in 2019, with a sharp increase from 2016 onward.
- This upward trend suggests rising demand for hotel and travel services, likely driven by improved accessibility, tourism growth, and digital booking adoption.

*Origin Markets*
- Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, and Indonesia are the top contributing countries, indicating that Southeast Asia represents the largest share of outbound bookings.

*Destination Markets*
- Bookings are concentrated among a limited number of destination countries and cities.
- London, Dublin, and Auckland attract the highest booking volumes among top destination cities, followed by Hamilton and Belo Horizonte.

*Stay Category*
- Short stays dominate booking volume compared to medium and long stays.
- Medium stays contribute moderately, while long stays make up the smallest share — indicating the business is primarily driven by short-term travelers.

*Age Group Analysis*
- The 30–50 age group records the highest booking volume, pointing to working-age traveler dominance.
- Younger (10–20) and older age brackets show significantly lower participation.

*Payment Mode Distribution*
- Payment methods are fairly evenly distributed (~459K–462K bookings each), with no single method overwhelmingly dominant.
- This reflects diverse customer payment preferences and strong support for multiple payment options.

Business Value

This analysis helps stakeholders in the hospitality and travel industry:
- Identify high-value source markets for targeted marketing
- Understand traveler demographics to tailor offerings
- Optimize payment infrastructure based on customer preferences
- Prioritize investment in top-performing destination cities

- Conclusion

The dashboard provides a clear view of hotel booking patterns and traveler behavior. The insights can help hospitality businesses understand customer segments, monitor booking trends, and make data-driven decisions about destinations and travel services.
